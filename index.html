Kaun Se Cultures Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain?

Pani, Compost ya Gobar Khad (FYM) me prayog ke samay milane ka sahi tarika:

Group 1: Sr. No. 1 – 11 Nutrients + Disease Control

Serial Number 1 se 11 Tak Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain

Azotobacter, Azospirillum, Acetobacter, Rhizobium, PSB, KMB, ZMB, SMB, IMB, SiMB, CMB, Pseudomonas fluorescens, Trichoderma viride aur Trichoderma harzianum ko prayog ke samay pani, compost ya FYM me ek sath milaya ja sakta hai.

Group 2: Sr. No. 12 – 14 & 12 – 15 Pests + Nematodes Control

Serial Number 12 – 14 aur 12 – 15 Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain

Metarhizium (12), Beauveria (13), Verticillium (14) ko aapas me aur Paecilomyces sp. (15) ke sath milakar prayog kar sakte hain. Nematodes ke liye Sr. No. 15 ko Sr. No. 9 aur 10 ke sath bhi milaya jata hai.

Organic Knowledge Library

Pests, Diseases & Jaivik Cultivation Tips

Disease Management (Sr. 9–11)

Uktha Rog (Wilt) & Jad Galan

Fasal me achanak paudha murjhana ya jad ka sadna fafund ke karan hota hai. Iske jaivik niyantran ke liye Trichoderma viride (10), T. harzianum (11) aur Pseudomonas (9) ko gobar khad (FYM) me milakar jadon ke paas dein.

Pest Management (Sr. 12–14)

Sundi, Safed Makhi & Deemak

Bina zehrile chemical spray ke keeton ko rokne ke liye Beauveria bassiana (13) aur Verticillium lecanii (14) ka patton par spray karein, aur mitti ke keeton (white grub/deemak) ke liye Metarhizium (12) dalein.

Nematode & Soil Tips (Sr. 15)

Jad Gaanth Rog (Nematodes)

Sabjiyon aur bagwani me jadon par gaanth banne par Paecilomyces sp. (15) ko Pseudomonas (9) aur Trichoderma viride (10) ke sath milakar 3–4 ghante ke andar sinchai ya compost ke sath dein.
