Complete Soil Science & Sustainable Management

Mitti Ko Samjhein: pH, EC, Organic Carbon Aur Soil Structure

Mitti sirf dhool nahi, ek jeeti-jaagti prayogshala hai. Jab mitti ka pH, EC aur Organic Carbon santulit rehta hai, tabhi Bio-Cultures 100% shakti se kaam karte hain.

1. Mitti Ki Aatma: Soil pH (Ideal: 6.5 – 7.5)

pH Ka 'Nutrient Lock' Vigyan Aur Upchar

• Acidic Soil (pH < 6.0): Low pH par Aluminium aur Iron zehrile star tak ghul jate hain aur Phosphorus, Calcium, Magnesium ko lock kar dete hain. Upchar: Buwai se 3–4 hafte pehle 1–2 ton/acre Agricultural Lime / Dolomite (Chuna) milayein aur PSB + CMB bio-cultures dein.

• Alkaline / Sodic Soil (pH > 7.5): High pH par Phosphorus calcium se judkar patthar (Tricalcium phosphate) ban jata hai aur Zinc, Iron, Manganese lock ho jate hain. Upchar: Gypsum (500 kg – 2 ton/acre) milakar bhari sinchai (leaching) karein, Dhaincha (Hari Khad) palat dein aur SMB + IMB + ZMB dein.

2. Mitti Ka Current: EC (Ideal: 0.0 – 0.8 dS/m)

Kharapan (Salinity) Aur Reverse Osmosis Se Bachav

• High EC (> 1.6 dS/m) Ka Khatra: Jab mitti me namak (Sodium, Chloride) zyada hota hai, to pani jadon me jaane ke bajay jadon se bahar khinchne lagta hai (Reverse Osmosis), jisse gili mitti me bhi paudha sookhne lagta hai aur pattiyon ke kinare jalte hain (Tip Burn).

• Jaivik Samadhan: Khet me achhi jal nikasi (Drainage) banakar meethay pani se Leaching karein, sadi gobar khad (FYM) aur Humic substance dein, aur jadon ki suraksha ke liye Trichoderma harzianum + Pseudomonas fluorescens ka prayog karein.

3. Mitti Ka Engine: Organic Carbon (Ideal: > 0.75%)

Jaivik Carbon Aur C:N Ratio Ka Rahasya

• 3-in-1 Fayde: Organic Carbon mitti me Sponge (pani rokne), Chumbak (poshak tatvon ko bahne se rokne), aur Microbes ka Bhojan banne ka kaam karta hai. Bina carbon ke koi bhi bio-culture zinda nahi reh sakta.

• C:N Anupat (10:1 se 12:1): Khet me kachha bhoosa ya parali kabhi na jalayein; us par gobar khad aur bio-cultures ka chhidkav karke khet me hi galayein. Hari khad (Dhaincha/Sanai) aur Vermicompost se OC tezi se badhta hai.

4. Mitti Ka Shareer: Sandy, Loam & Clay Management

Mitti Ki Banawat Ke Anusar Jaivik Prabandhan

• Retili Mitti (Sandy Soil): Pani aur khad jaldi neeche beh (Leach) jate hain. Isme 5–10 ton/acre sadi FYM/Compost milayein aur bio-cultures ko 2–3 baar tukdon (Split dose) me dein.

• Chikni / Kali Mitti (Clay Soil): Pani bharne se jadon ko oxygen nahi milti aur Phosphorus lock hota hai. Geeli mitti me rotavator na chalayein, Gypsum aur PSB + KMB + Silicon (SiMB) ka prayog karein. Domat (Loam) mitti sabse uttam hoti hai.

Upyog Vidhi & Multiplication Formula

1 Liter Mother Culture Se 30 Liter Banane Ki Aasan Vidhi

1 Acre khet me multiply kiya hua 1 Liter culture dalta hai. Har culture ko 3 baar multiply karke 5 se 7 din me 1 Liter se 30 Liter tak taiyar kiya ja sakta hai!

Step 1

Gud Aur Pani Ubaalein

10 Liter pani me 300 gram gud (jaggery) milakar achhi tarah ubaalein jab tak gud pani me poori tarah ghul na jaye.

Step 2

Chhaan Kar Thanda Karein

Is gud ke ghol ko ek saaf Markin ke kapde (clean Markin cloth) se chhaan lein aur room temperature par thanda hone dein.

Step 3

Besan (Gram Flour) Milayein

Jab ghol thanda ho jaye, tab usme 100 gram besan (gram flour) achhi tarah mila dein.

Step 4

1 Liter Mother Culture Daalein

Ab is 10 Liter gud-besan ke ghol me 1 Liter Mother Culture milayein, saaf Markin ke kapde se muh dhak dein aur chhaon (shade) me rakhein.

Step 5

Rozana Dande Se Hilayein

3 se 6 feet lambe lakdi ya steel ke dande se din me 1–2 baar hilayein. Bacterial cultures me pehle din se roz hilayein, aur Fungal cultures me 4 din baad roz hilayein.

Step 6 (5–7 Din)

1 Hafte Me Taiyar (3 Baar Multiply)

5–7 din (1 hafta) me culture taiyar ho jayega. Isse 10L banega aur is process ko 3 baar dohrayein to 1 Liter se 30 Liter ban jayega! (1 Acre = 1 Liter prayog karein).

Category-Wise Cultures & Mixing Guide

Kaun Sa Culture Kis Kaam Aata Hai Aur Kaise Milayein

1. Nutrients Fixation & Mobilization (₹400/L)

Poshan Dene Wale Cultures:

• Nitrogen Fixing Bacteria (Azotobacter, Azospirillum, Acetobacter, Rhizobium)
• Phosphorus Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB)
• Potash Mobilizing Bacteria (KMB)
• Zinc Mobilizing Bacteria (ZMB)
• Sulphur Mobilizing Bacteria (SMB)
• Iron Mobilizing Bacteria (IMB)
• Silicon Mobilizing Bacteria (SiMB)
• Calcium Mobilizing Bacteria (CMB)

2. Disease Management / Control (₹420/L)

Bimari Aur Fafund Rokne Wale Cultures:

• Pseudomonas fluorescens
• Trichoderma viride
• Trichoderma harzianum

4. Plant Parasitic Nematodes Control

• Paecilomyces sp. ko Pseudomonas fluorescens aur Trichoderma viride ke sath milakar jad gaanth (nematodes) control ke liye prayog karein.

3. Pests Management / Control (₹440/L)

Keet Niyantran Karne Wale Cultures:

• Metarhizium anisopliae (Safed lat, deemak)
• Beauveria bassiana (Sundi, whitefly, thrips)
• Verticillium lecanii (Mahu, jassids, mealybug)

Aapas Me Milane (Mixing) Ke Niyam

Pani, Compost Ya FYM Me Milane Ka Sahi Tarika:

• Group A (Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain): Sabhi Nutrients Cultures (Nitrogen fixing, PSB, KMB, ZMB, SMB, IMB, SiMB, CMB) aur Disease Control Cultures (Pseudomonas fluorescens, Trichoderma viride, Trichoderma harzianum) ko prayog ke samay ek sath milaya ja sakta hai.

• Group B (Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain): Metarhizium anisopliae, Beauveria bassiana, Verticillium lecanii ko aapas me aur Paecilomyces sp. ke sath milakar prayog kar sakte hain.

⏱️ Zaroori Niyam: Cultures ko keval prayog ke samay hi milayein aur 3 se 4 ghante ke andar khet me istemal kar lein. Mix karne ke baad store karke na rakhein.
