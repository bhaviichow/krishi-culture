Upyog Vidhi • Multiply Up to 30×

1 Liter Mother Culture Se 30 Liter Banane Ki Vidhi (5–7 Din)

1 Acre khet me multiply kiya hua 1 Liter culture dalta hai. Is process ko 3 baar dohrakar 1 Liter se 30 Liter (30 Acre ke liye) bana sakte hain.

1. Gud-Pani Ubaalein: 10 Liter pani me 300 gram gud (jaggery) milakar achhi tarah ubaalein jab tak poori tarah ghul jaye.
2. Markin Kapde Se Chhaanein: Ghol ko saaf Markin ke kapde se chhaan kar room temperature par thanda hone dein.
3. Besan Milayein: Thanda hone par isme 100 gram besan (gram flour) achhi tarah ghol dein.
4. Mother Culture & Chhaon: 1 Liter Mother Culture milakar Markin kapde se muh bandhein aur chhaon (shade) me rakhein.
5. Dande Se Hilayein: 3–6 ft lakdi/steel dande se din me 1–2 baar hilayein (Bacterial: pehle din se roz; Fungal: 4 din baad roz).
6. 1 Hafte Me Taiyar (3× Repeat): 5–7 din me taiyar culture ko 1L bottles me chhaon me rakhein; 3 baar multiply se 30L taiyar!

Cultures Ko Aapas Me Milane (Mixing) Ke Sahi Niyam

Poshan aur Rog Niyantran Combo (Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain):

Nitrogen Fixing Bacteria (Azotobacter, Azospirillum, Acetobacter, Rhizobium), PSB, KMB, ZMB, SMB, IMB, SiMB, CMB, Pseudomonas fluorescens, Trichoderma viride aur Trichoderma harzianum ko prayog ke samay ek sath milaya ja sakta hai.

Keet aur Nematodes Combo (Ek Sath Mila Sakte Hain):

Metarhizium anisopliae, Beauveria bassiana aur Verticillium lecanii ko aapas me aur Paecilomyces sp. ke sath mila sakte hain. Nematodes ke liye Paecilomyces sp. ko Pseudomonas fluorescens aur Trichoderma viride ke sath milakar chalayein.

⏱️ Zaroori Savdhani: Cultures ko pani, compost ya gobar khad (FYM) me keval prayog ke samay hi milayein aur 3 se 4 ghante ke andar khet me upyog kar lein. Mix karne ke baad lambe samay tak store na karein.
